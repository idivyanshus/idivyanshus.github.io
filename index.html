03 · How I think

How I think about marketing

Four beliefs I keep coming back to. Each one is really about how people behave, and each one came from something I've actually done.

01 The psychology: context

Meet people where they already are.

Nobody goes looking for a brand. People notice you when you show up in a space they already care about, speaking their language. When we wanted Stake players to try our games, I didn't push the old company account harder. I built a page made only for them.

Proof: 25K+ impressions in 4 days →
02 The psychology: reciprocity

Give first, then ask.

When you help someone first, they want to help you back. Instead of paying affiliates for listings, I gave them value: a do-follow link with their logo on our partners page, articles about them that I wrote for our news section, and shoutouts on our socials for partners who pushed our games. And I stay in touch with them, with new game updates and news, long after they go live.

Proof: 80+ affiliates live with $0 in listing fees, and 3 awards for our games →
03 The psychology: word of mouth

Make it worth talking about.

A discount gets a sale today and is forgotten tomorrow. A story gets retold at the dinner table. That's why I gave kids a colour book and a contest to enter, and put up gossip posters near colleges, instead of another price drop, and why, with no budget, we traded for live singers and hosted a biker meetup. You don't need money to be memorable. You need a reason for people to talk.

Proof: about 150% sales lift in a week, and ₹0 extra spend on activations →
04 The psychology: behaviour over assumption

Numbers show what happened. People explain why.

I check the numbers after every send and every post, and I change the next piece of content based on what they show. But a number only tells you where to look. Working out why people reacted that way is the part that makes the next idea better.

Proof: every newsletter and post shapes the next one →

06 / 08F&B · FAMILY EXPERIENCE

Colour Book: keeping kids busy, and families coming back

0%more family footfall (approx.)
The brief
Families with small kids found the wait for food hard, and a plain discount wouldn't give them a reason to come back.
The move
A foldable four-page colour book with cartoon drawings and our branding, plus colours, for every child aged 1 to 6. Parents coloured with their kids instead of rushing. Then a weekly contest: post the finished book with your child, tag us, and the best one wins a free meal. Rolled out at every outlet.
The result
About 60% more family footfall, and a steady flow of parents tagging us on social media every week.
